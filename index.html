Why a second round of testing? In the first round, all 10 unseen images were classified correctly — no failure cases were found. Since a flawless result on only 10 images could be coincidental, a second, larger round was run with 40 unseen images to check whether a bigger sample would reveal any failure cases. The result was stable: the 40 unseen images were still all classified correctly, again yielding 100% accuracy with no misclassifications.
